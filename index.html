Questions

Straight answers.

Is the 3D design really free?

Yes. The design and the in-home consultation cost nothing, and there's no obligation to move forward. We do it because seeing the design is the best way for you to decide.

Why are spots limited?

Every design is made by hand for your specific yard. We can only do a set number each month and still do them well, so we cap it.

What areas do you serve?

The San Fernando Valley, West LA, the rest of Los Angeles, and Orange County. Not sure? Submit the form and we'll tell you.

What do I need to get started?

Just the form. We'll text you to ask for a few photos of your yard and what you want to use it for.

Do I have to build with you?

No. The design is yours to keep. If you love it and want help bringing it to life, we'll talk about that at the consultation.

Are the images on this site real projects?

They're 3D design concepts, the same kind of visual you'll get for your own yard. Each one is labeled "Design concept."
