Scope & price

Deep clean (power wash)

Pre-treat dirt, moss and algae, then clean with a commercial surface cleaner for an even finish. Edges, steps and joints detailed; full rinse.

Re-sand joints (pavers)

Clear old sand and weeds. Once dry, sweep in polymeric sand, compact so it fills every gap, top off and mist to lock it in. Helps stop weeds, ants and shifting.

Sealer

Applied on a fully dry surface (usually a second visit). Even coats, edges cut in by hand. Brings back color and guards against stains and fading.

Stain treatment

Degreaser or poultice on each oil, rust or grease spot. Most lighten a lot; deep, old stains may not fully disappear.
