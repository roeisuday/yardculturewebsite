Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Yard Culture SoCal ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you submit in our forms: name, phone, email, ZIP code, city, and details about your project.
  • Basic analytics and advertising data, such as pages visited and the ad or link that brought you here (including through the Meta Pixel and UTM tags).

How we use it

  • To prepare your free 3D design and schedule your consultation.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request. Text messages are sent only if you consent, and you can reply STOP at any time.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our business, including our CRM (HighLevel), website hosting (Cloudflare) and advertising platforms (Meta). They process data on our behalf.
